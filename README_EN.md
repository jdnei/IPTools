# IP & Network Diagnostic Toolkit (Updated November 2, 2026)

> A collection of useful tools for IP lookup, geolocation, BGP routing, risk and reputation analysis, privacy leak detection, network connectivity testing, speed testing, and public-facing asset inspection.
>
> On-site navigation, promotional entry points, and internal links have been removed. Tool descriptions are brief summaries; actual results depend on the respective services.

## Usage Tips

- Compare your public exit IP before and after enabling a VPN or proxy to avoid confusing your local network with the proxy server.
- IP geolocation and risk scores may vary between databases. Cross-check results from multiple sources.
- When troubleshooting network issues, compare Ping, MTR, and TCP results across different times and internet service providers.
- DNS, WebRTC, IPv6 leaks, and IP reputation are separate aspects of network security and should be tested individually.
- Public-facing asset discovery tools should only be used on systems you own or are explicitly authorized to inspect.

## VPS / Proxy Exit Troubleshooting Workflow

1. **Identify Your Current Exit IP:** Use [Ping0.cc](https://ping0.cc/) or [IP.SKK](https://ip.skk.moe/) to check your public IP address, ASN, and network type.

2. **Cross-Check IP Reputation:** Compare information from [Scamalytics](https://scamalytics.com/ip), [AbuseIPDB](https://www.abuseipdb.com/), and other sources. A single risk score cannot guarantee access to a specific service.

3. **Verify Network Ownership:** Use [BGP.Tools](https://bgp.tools/) or similar services to inspect the autonomous system and upstream providers. A single ASN is not sufficient to determine the overall quality of a network route.

4. **Test Network Routes:** Use [ITDOG](https://www.itdog.cn/) and [tcp.cm](https://tcp.cm/) to test latency, routing, and port connectivity from multiple locations.

5. **Check for Privacy Leaks:** Use [IPLeak](https://ipleak.net/) or [DNS Leak Test](https://www.dnsleaktest.com/) to verify DNS, WebRTC, and IPv6 behavior.

> **Note:** Results from these websites are troubleshooting indicators, not guarantees that an account, AI service, or streaming platform will work. Some services may require login or payment, or may be inaccessible due to regional and network restrictions.
